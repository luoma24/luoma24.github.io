rclone-desktop

这是一个个人自用的 OAuth 应用，仅用于通过 rclone 访问应用所有者本人的 Google Drive。它不对外提供服务，也没有其他用户。

This is a personal OAuth application used solely by its owner to access their own Google Drive through rclone. It is not a public service and has no other users.

权限范围 / Scope

仅申请 drive 权限，用于读写本人 Drive 中的文件。/ Only the drive scope is requested.
